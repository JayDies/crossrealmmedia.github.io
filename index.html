1. Four realms, played four ways

We don’t port one game four times. We design each version around how that platform’s players play.

  • Fortnite

    Islands built in Unreal Editor for Fortnite, scripted in Verse.

  • Roblox

    Experiences and minigames, scripted in Luau.

  • Minecraft

    Bedrock add-ons, Java mods, maps and minigames.

  • Standalone

    Our own PC and browser games in Godot, on itch.io first, then Steam.

3. Two people and a crew of agents

  1. Humans make the calls

    What to build, what feels right, what ships.

  2. Agents do the legwork

    Design drafts, code, QA passes, art direction and research.

  3. Playtests come early

    Players try rough builds, so we can test lots of ideas fast and keep the good ones.
